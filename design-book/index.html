00 · The brief

A softer way into a PDF.

Readwell is a public website whose main event is opening a PDF and settling into reading. The brand should feel aesthetic, simple, and light. Visual warmth belongs around the document; the page itself stays calm, spacious, and legible.

The design decision

Recommend Quiet Paper: a warm off-white canvas, high-contrast ink, and a deep roast-brown action color, supported by quiet linen, soft sage, and a restrained editorial type pairing. Keep the reading surface bright and uncluttered.

What this is—and is not

This is a design exploration and specification. Current implementation is still Folio. Readwell, its public welcome shell, default Light appearance, detailed layouts, and exact tokens are proposals; they do not change production behavior.

Evidence used with care

Quiet Paper takes the warm option while keeping the same restraint principle: low visual noise, softly muted supporting colors, and unmistakably dark reading ink. Color and coffee are aesthetic cues, not promises that a palette will calm every reader or improve cognition. Ink on canvas measures 12.39:1; white on the roast-brown action measures 7.74:1, above WCAG 2.1 AA's 4.5:1 normal-text threshold.

Menus, dialogs, and navigation

Toolbar controls

Keep Book / Original PDF and Page flip / Scroll as mutually exclusive segmented controls with a programmatic selected state. Zoom buttons remain separate and labeled. Bookmark toggle exposes “Add bookmark” / “Remove bookmark.”

Menus and popovers

Open beside their trigger, align to the control group, fit within the viewport, and close on Escape or outside click. Keep focus inside modal dialogs; restore it to the invoking control on close.

Contents navigation

Chapters are links with a visible current location, human-readable title, and page number. On mobile, open in a drawer with an explicit close action, Escape support, and a scrim that does not obscure the title.

Reduced motion

Use 160ms control feedback and 180ms dialog transitions; page turn may take up to 240ms in the proposal. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, remove animated page travel and move to the destination immediately.

07 · Interaction & access

Keep the interface comfortable to use.

Keyboard path

  • Skip link first, then brand, open action, contents, reading controls, page, and progress.
  • Tab moves among controls; arrow keys move within a segmented control and contents list.
  • Escape closes popover, dialog, or contents drawer and restores focus.
  • PageUp / PageDown navigate in Page flip; standard scrolling remains available in Scroll mode.

Screen reader feedback

  • Announce document title, current page, total pages, and selected layout.
  • Use a polite live region for loading progress; announce errors once and expose a recovery action.
  • Icon-only controls need accessible names and pressed/expanded state.
  • Respect browser-local behavior; do not imply a cloud upload or account.

Responsive rules

  • Text and columns grow; paper width stops growing at the defined max.
  • Chapter titles wrap, never truncate critical navigation.
  • On touch, targets are at least 44×44 CSS px; visible gaps reduce accidental taps.
  • Focus ring remains visible above page and drawer layers.

Verification boundary

Contrast values in this book were calculated for the listed foreground/background pairs. Browser, screen-reader, keyboard, touch-device, and assistive-technology behavior of these proposed mockups has not been certified as production behavior.
